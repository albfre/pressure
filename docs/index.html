About

The pressure optimizer finds the order in which to connect donor tubes to target tubes to get an efficient gas transfer. It calculates the optimal sequence of transfers to achieve a pressure as close as possible to (but not exceeding) the maximum pressure of the target tube.

How to interpret the results

  • Final pressure: These show the resulting pressures in each tube after all transfers are complete.
  • Connections to make: This table shows the sequence of transfers to perform:
    • D# → T#: Indicates a transfer from donor tube # to target tube #
    • Pressures before/after: Shows the pressures in the donor and target tubes before and after each transfer

Follow the connections in order to achieve the optimal transfer of gas.

Optimization specifics

The optimization is performed using explicit enumeration of possible connections, using some symmetries to avoid having to enumerate redundant combinations. The objective function, which is to be minimized, is lexicographic:

  1. Worst case target: The difference between the maximum pressure and the final pressure of the worst case target.
  2. Number of connections: The number of steps in the sequence of connections to make.
  3. Average difference: The average of the differences between the maximum pressure and the final pressure for all targets.
